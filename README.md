# TrueGrade — Trust is not claimed. It is measured.

> **Institutional Authority meets Rural Warmth.**  
> AI-powered agricultural grading and agri-fintech platform bridging farmers, mandis, and financial institutions with tamper-evident Digital Quality Certificates (DQCs).

---

## 🌾 Overview

TrueGrade transforms physical grain inspection into verifiable, cryptographically signed digital collateral. By replacing 14-day manual assayer delays with instantaneous optical scanning and sub-millimeter NIR spectral sensing, TrueGrade unlocks fair mandi prices for farmers and empowers banks and NBFCs with zero-haircut collateral verification for 60-second credit disbursements.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **The Truth Box**: A floating glassmorphic hero component featuring real-time telemetry (moisture, protein, infestation index, spectral signature, macro-optics ID), SVG Trust Score gauge, and digital QR certification.
2. **Farmer's Market Intelligence**: Live Mandi price discovery mirroring AGMARKNET with AI-driven **Hold** vs **Sell** recommendations calculated from price volatility.
3. **Lend-Link API Bridge**: A dedicated banker console with a 60-second countdown timer, collateral verification, and one-click loan disbursement execution.
4. **The Optical Moat**: Interactive 50-micron macro grain photometry demonstration with mouse-responsive laser scanning and anti-tamper hardware geofencing.
5. **Transparency Metrics**: Live ecosystem KPI dashboard tracking cumulative facilitated value, collateral velocity, active grain lots, and average trust scores.
6. **Multi-Language Support**: Interactive language switcher supporting English, हिन्दी (Hindi), ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi), मराठी (Marathi), and తెలుగు (Telugu).
